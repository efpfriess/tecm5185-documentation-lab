# Submitting an Application

Follow these steps to submit your application.

## Before You Begin

- Gather your transcripts.
- Prepare your personal statement.

## Procedure

1. Sign in to the application portal.
2. Complete the required fields.
3. Review your answers.
4. Submit your application.
5. Verify the application. 
6. Email the applicant.

## After You Submit
## After You Submit

After submitting your application:

1. Save your confirmation number.
2. Check your email for requests for additional information.
3. Check your application portal for outstanding requirements.
Visit [the tech comm website](https://techcomm.unt.edu)

**Important:** Save a copy of your confirmation.
